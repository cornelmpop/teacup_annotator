1. In **Open last folder**, click **OK**.
2. In **Choose annotation classes**, choose **Use defaults**.
3. In **Use model.conf?**, click **Yes**. Wait until the project shows **9 images**.
4. Click an empty part of the image canvas once to give it keyboard focus.
5. With the pointer over the canvas, hold **Z**, make **four distinct upward scroll gestures**, then release **Z**. Confirm the image zooms in.
6. Without holding a key, make **two distinct downward scroll gestures** over the canvas. Confirm the image pans vertically.
7. Click **Reset zoom**. Wait for the image to fit the canvas again.
8. Click **Show labels**. Confirm yellow class labels appear on annotations.
9. Click **Show overlapping edges**. Confirm its checkbox becomes selected; no visible edge change is required.
10. Under **Enable snapping for**, click **new**, then **edits**. Confirm both checkboxes become selected.
11. Click **Turtle shell mode**.
12. In the **Turtle shell mode** notice, click **OK**. Confirm the checkbox is selected.
13. Click **Turtle shell mode** again to turn it off. No dialog is expected.
14. Click **Show labels** and **Show overlapping edges** again to turn both off.
15. Click **new** and **edits** again to turn both snapping options off.
16. Click **Save** and wait for saving to complete.
17. Click the macOS red window-close button. No close-time prompt is expected.

When finished, report completion and every deviation—even an accidental click, extra keystroke, unexpected dialog, or observable result that differed from these instructions.
