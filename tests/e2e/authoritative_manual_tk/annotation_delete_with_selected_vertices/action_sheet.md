# Annotation delete with selected vertices

Human recording performed against the `master_2` real-Tk fixture.

1. In **Open last folder**, click **OK**.
2. In **Choose annotation classes**, choose **Use annotation/default classes**.
3. In **Use model.conf?**, click **Yes** and wait for the project to load.
4. Press Right Arrow exactly five times, waiting for each image to load; confirm that the index field shows 6.
5. Click a visibly non-rectangular annotation and confirm that it is selected.
6. Hold `a` down.
7. While holding `a`, left-drag across a short run of vertices, release the mouse first, continue holding `a`, and confirm that the vertices are selected.
8. Release `a` once, do not press it again, and confirm that the vertex selection remains.
9. Press `d` and confirm that the entire annotation disappears.
10. Click **Save** and wait for it to finish.
11. Close the window using the macOS close control. If an unexpected prompt appears, click **Cancel** and report it.
