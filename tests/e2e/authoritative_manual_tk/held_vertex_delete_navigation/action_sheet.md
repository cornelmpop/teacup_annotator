# Held vertex-delete key across navigation

Human recording performed against the `master_2` real-Tk fixture.

1. In **Open last folder**, click **OK**.
2. In **Choose annotation classes**, choose **Use annotation/default classes**.
3. In **Use model.conf?**, click **Yes** and wait for the project to load.
4. Press Right Arrow exactly five times, waiting for each image to load; confirm that the index field shows 6.
5. Click a visibly non-rectangular annotation and confirm that it is selected.
6. Hold `a` down.
7. While holding `a`, left-drag across a short run of vertices, release the mouse while continuing to hold `a`, and confirm that the vertices are selected.
8. Still holding `a`, press `Return`; confirm that the vertex highlights disappear and the annotation redraws.
9. Still holding `a`, press Right Arrow twice and wait for both image changes.
10. Still holding `a`, press Left Arrow twice and wait until the index field returns to 6.
11. Release `a` and wait.
12. Click empty canvas space and confirm that nothing is selected.
13. Click **Save** and wait for it to finish.
14. Close the window using the macOS close control. If an unexpected prompt appears, click **Cancel** and report it.
