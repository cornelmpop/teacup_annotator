1. In **Open last folder**, click **OK**.
2. In **Choose annotation classes**, choose **Use defaults**.
3. In **Use model.conf?**, click **Yes**. Wait until the project shows **9 images**.
4. If the snapping checkboxes are not visible, resize the main window by dragging its lower-right corner until the complete **Controls** panel is visible.
5. Click the image-number field, replace its contents with **2**, and press **Return**. Wait for the empty second image.
6. Click an empty part of the canvas once to give it keyboard focus.
7. Under **Enable snapping for**, click **new**, then **edits**. Confirm both are selected.
8. Click **Turtle shell mode**.
9. In the **Turtle shell mode** notice, click **OK**. Confirm the mode is selected.
10. Press **Q** once to start a polygon.
11. Near the center-left of the canvas, create a large four-corner polygon:

    - Click its top-left corner.
    - Click its bottom-left corner.
    - Click its bottom-right corner.
    - Click its top-right corner.
    - Click the original top-left vertex to close it.

    Wait until one completed polygon is visible.
12. Press **Q** once to start another polygon immediately to the right of the first.
13. Create the adjoining polygon as follows:

    - Click exactly on the first polygon’s **top-right vertex**.
    - Click a point farther right at approximately the same height.
    - Click another point farther right at approximately the height of the first polygon’s bottom edge.
    - Click exactly on the first polygon’s **bottom-right vertex**.

    Do not click the starting vertex again. Wait for Turtle shell mode to close the polygon along the first polygon’s right edge. Confirm that two completed adjoining polygons are visible.
14. Click **Show overlapping edges**. Confirm it becomes selected; if a coloured shared edge appears between the polygons, note it, but its visibility is not required.
15. Left-click once inside the **left polygon** to select it. Wait for its vertex handles to appear.
16. Drag the shared **top-right vertex** a short distance upward and left. Confirm both polygon outlines remain joined at the moved vertex.
17. Click **Turtle shell mode** to turn it off.
18. Left-click once inside the left polygon again to ensure it is selected.
19. Hold **W**. While holding it, drag the shared **bottom-right vertex** a short distance downward and left, then release **W**. Confirm both polygon outlines remain joined at the moved vertex.
20. Click **Show overlapping edges** to turn it off.
21. Under **Enable snapping for**, click **new**, then **edits**, turning both off.
22. Click **Save** and wait for saving to complete.
23. Click the macOS red window-close button. No close-time prompt is expected.

When finished, report completion and every deviation, including any vertex that failed to snap, unexpected closure behavior, extra click, or different visible result.
