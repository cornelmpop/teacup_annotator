# Menu pointer and resize causality workflow

Human recording performed against the `master_2` real-Tk fixture.

1. In **Open last folder**, click **OK**.
2. In **Choose annotation classes**, choose **Use annotation/default classes**.
3. In **Use model.conf?**, click **Yes**. Wait until the project is fully loaded and the canvas is visible.
4. Click once with the left mouse button in an empty part of the canvas to give the canvas keyboard focus.
5. Hold down **z**. While continuing to hold **z**, move the mouse wheel upward by four distinct notches over the canvas. Release **z** and wait for the zoomed view to settle.
6. Press and fully release **r** once. Wait until rectangle-creation mode is active.
7. In an empty, comfortably sized part of the image, drag with the left mouse button to create one obvious rectangle. Release the mouse button and wait for the finished rectangle to appear.
8. Place the pointer in empty canvas space immediately to the right of the new rectangle—not on the rectangle or an existing annotation. **Control-click at that location**, wait for the context menu to appear, then choose **Undo**. Wait until the rectangle disappears.
9. Press and fully release **q** once. Wait until polygon-creation mode is active.
10. Left-click near the central area of the image to place the first polygon vertex.
11. Left-click above and to the left of the first vertex to place the second polygon vertex.
12. Drag the macOS window’s lower-right corner outward by a noticeable amount. Release it and wait until the canvas has completely redrawn.
13. Left-click below and to the left of the first vertex to place the third polygon vertex.
14. Press and release **Escape** once. Wait until the unfinished polygon disappears and normal mode returns.
15. Click **Save** once. Wait until saving finishes and the interface is responsive.
16. Click the macOS red window-close button. No close-time prompt is expected; if one appears, note its exact title and buttons and report it as a deviation.
