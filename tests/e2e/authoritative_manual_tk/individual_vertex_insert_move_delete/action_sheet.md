### Individual vertex insertion and deletion

1. In **Open last folder**, click **OK**.
2. In **Choose annotation classes**, choose **Defaults**.
3. In **Use model.conf?**, click **Yes**; wait for image 1 of 9 to load fully.
4. Enter `2` in the image-number field and press Return; wait for the empty second image.
5. Click the canvas, then press `Q`.
6. Draw a large free polygon with at least eight well-separated vertices and long straight edges. Click its first vertex to close it.
7. Click inside the polygon to select it.
8. Left-click near the midpoint of a long edge, away from existing vertices. Confirm that a new vertex appears.
9. Left-drag that new vertex outward. Confirm that the outline follows it.
10. Middle-click a different vertex. Confirm that the vertex disappears.
11. Middle-button press another vertex, move the pointer a short distance while holding the button, then release. Confirm that this vertex also disappears.
12. Click **Save** and wait for completion.
13. Close using the macOS window close button. Report any prompt or deviation.
