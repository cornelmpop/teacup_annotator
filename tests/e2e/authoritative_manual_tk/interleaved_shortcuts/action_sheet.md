# Interleaved shortcut workflow

Human recording performed against the `master_2` real-Tk fixture.

1. In **Open last folder**, click **OK**.
2. In **Choose annotation classes**, choose **Use annotation/default classes**.
3. In **Use model.conf?**, click **Yes** and wait for the project to load.
4. Put the pointer over the canvas, press `q`, and wait for the crosshair cursor.
5. Press `Escape` and confirm that the normal cursor returns.
6. Press `r`, wait for the crosshair, press `Escape`, and confirm that the normal cursor returns.
7. Press `a` and wait for the crosshair.
8. Click empty canvas space and move slightly so the arrow preview appears.
9. Press `Escape` and confirm that the preview disappears.
10. Hold `x`, drag a broad left-button box enclosing at least two annotations, release the mouse, then release `x`; confirm at least two annotations are selected.
11. Click empty canvas space and confirm that the selection clears.
12. Click a visibly non-rectangular annotation and confirm that it is selected.
13. Press `s` and confirm that the redrawn annotation remains selected.
14. Press `d` and confirm that the annotation disappears.
15. Click **Save** and wait for it to finish.
16. Close the window using the macOS close control. If an unexpected prompt appears, click **Cancel** and report it.
