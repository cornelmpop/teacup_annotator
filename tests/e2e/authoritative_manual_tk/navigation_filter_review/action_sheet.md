4. Click the image-index field, press `Command+A`, type `6`, and press Return. Wait until the counter shows `6 / 9`.
5. Open the class-filter dropdown, select `lithics`, and click **Filter by class**. Wait until the counter shows `4 / 4`.
6. Click the toolbar flag button beside **Reset zoom**; confirm its icon changes to indicate the current image is flagged.
7. Click **Previous**; wait until the counter shows `3 / 4`.
8. Click the flag button; confirm this image is also flagged.
9. Click **View flagged**. Wait until the counter shows `1 / 2` and the button changes to **View all**.
10. Click **Next**; wait until the counter shows `2 / 2`.
11. Click the flag button to remove the current image’s flag. Wait until it disappears from the filtered list and the counter becomes `1 / 1`.
12. Click **View all**. Wait until the `lithics`-filtered list returns and the counter shows `3 / 4`.
13. Click the flag button to remove the remaining review flag.
14. Open the class-filter dropdown, select `all`, and click **Filter by class**. Wait until all 9 images are available.
15. Click **Save** and wait for completion.
16. Close the main window using the macOS red button. No prompt is expected; if one appears, stop.

If any expected counter differs, stop at that step and report it. Otherwise, after closing, reply with `no deviations` or list every deviation.
