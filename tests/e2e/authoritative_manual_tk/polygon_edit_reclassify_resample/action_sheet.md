### Recording 8A — Polygon edit, reclassification, and resampling

1. In **Open last folder**, click **OK**.
2. In **Choose annotation classes**, choose **Use annotation/default classes**.
3. In **Use model.conf?**, click **Yes**; wait until 9 images load.
4. Click the image-index field, press `Command+A`, type `2`, and press Return. Wait for `2 / 9`; this image should have no existing annotations.
5. Move the pointer into the upper-left quarter of the image and press `Q`.
6. Create a medium-sized four-corner polygon:

   - Click its upper-left corner.
   - Click its upper-right corner.
   - Click its lower-right corner.
   - Click its lower-left corner.
   - Click the original upper-left point to close it.

7. Wait for the completed polygon, then click its interior; confirm that it becomes selected and vertex handles appear.
8. Drag the polygon’s upper-right vertex a short distance farther right; release and confirm that the polygon remains selected.
9. Control-click inside the polygon, open **Change class**, and choose `table`; confirm that its outline color changes.
10. Control-click inside the polygon again and choose **Resample outline**; wait for the resampled polygon to remain selected.
11. Click **Save** and wait for completion.
12. Close the main window using the macOS red button. No prompt is expected; if one appears, stop.

After closing, reply with `no deviations` or list every deviation.
