### Shortcuts from non-canvas controls

1. In **Open last folder**, click **OK**.
2. In **Choose annotation classes**, choose **Defaults**.
3. In **Use model.conf?**, click **Yes**; wait for image 1 of 9 to load fully.
4. Click **Next** and wait for image 2. Do not click the canvas afterward. Moving the pointer over the canvas without clicking is permitted if needed to expose the current drawing cursor.
5. With focus still on **Next**, press `Q`. Confirm polygon-drawing mode starts.
6. Press `Escape` to cancel; wait for normal mode.
7. Click **Turtle shell mode** to enable it; in the **Turtle shell mode** notice, click **OK**.
8. Without clicking the canvas, press `Q`. Confirm polygon mode starts while the checkbox retains focus.
9. Press `Escape` to cancel; wait for normal mode.
10. Click **Turtle shell mode** again to disable it.
11. Without clicking the canvas, press `A`. Confirm arrow mode starts.
12. Click twice in an empty image area to draw a short arrow; wait for redraw.
13. Click **Save** and wait for completion.
14. Close using the macOS window close button. Report any prompt or deviation.

Required result: `Q` works from **Next** and **Turtle shell mode** focus, `Escape` cancels both incomplete polygons, and `A` works from **Turtle shell mode** focus and produces a persistent arrow.
